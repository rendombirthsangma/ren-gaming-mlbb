🎮 Tournament Rules & Regulations

Official rules supplied by REN GAMING • North-east India Esports WGH ML.08.

📅 Important Dates

Registration: 26 September 2026

Last Date: 26 October 2026

Match Start: 28 October 2026

Match Time: 6:00 PM – 10:00 PM

🏆 Tournament

Online MLBB 5v5 tournament.

Entry: FREE

1st Prize: ₹1,000

Qualification: 2 wins in the first round.

🔄 Rejoin

Fee: ₹50 per team.

Available only until the First Round is completely finished. Teams must contact REN GAMING and complete payment before acceptance.

👥 Roster

Each team registers 5 main players. Substitute players may be included according to organizer instructions.

Only registered players may participate; one player cannot represent multiple teams.

🎮 Match Rules

Matches use an MLBB Custom Room. Room ID and password are provided before matches. Teams should be ready at least 15 minutes before their scheduled match.

⭐ Special Awards

Best Assist: highest assists in a single match.

Best Killer: highest kills in a single match.

Both receive a Weekly Diamond Pass and are confirmed after the tournament.

📜 Fair Play & Disputes

Hacking, cheating, scripting, map hacks, unauthorized third-party applications, toxic/abusive behavior, harassment, match-fixing, collusion, intentional feeding and result manipulation are prohibited.

Disputes should be reported to the REN GAMING administrator promptly with genuine screenshots, recordings, match results or other evidence. Final dispute decisions are made by the REN GAMING tournament administration.
